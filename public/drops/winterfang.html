How to read these odds

Chance of at least one copy per kill or chest opening, at players 1 and 0% magic find. Quest kills have separate odds.

Area sources use each zone’s monster list; named bosses have separate entries. Shrines and special spawns can add monsters. Level comparisons vary monster level and treasure class while keeping the monster and difficulty fixed. Comparison levels need not occur in that zone. Normal monsters use their own levels.
