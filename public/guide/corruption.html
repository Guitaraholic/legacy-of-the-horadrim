Corruption

Risk a permanent change to your equipment. The Hellforge Ember marks it Corrupted and applies a weighted outcome in the same transmute.

Hellforge Ember

Hellforge Ember

Consumed when it corrupts eligible equipment. It can improve the item, leave only the Corrupted mark, or ruin it with penalties.

Where it drops
  • Champions · Plain monsters · Hell · 0.6667% (1 in 150)
  • Unique monsters · Hell · 0.6506% (1 in 154)
  • Stone map and Descent champions · Stone map and Descent monsters · Hell · 2%
  • Stone map and Descent unique packs · Hell · 1.952%

How to corrupt

Put the item and the Ember in the Horadric Cube, then press Transmute. The item keeps its existing rolls, sockets and socket contents.

Finish crafting first

Corruption is final. A second Ember and cubing the item alone do nothing. Later Cube recipes refuse Corrupted equipment without consuming ingredients: rerolls, crafts, exchanges, base upgrades, socket adding and socket clearing. Repair and recharge remain allowed.

Finish socket work and gem feeding first. The Eye of Reckoning cannot reroll a Corrupted item; it also refuses gem-fed uniques before corruption.

Eligible equipment

Armour, weapons, rings and amulets use the groups below. Charms have no Ember recipe. Unsocketed low-quality, normal and superior bases cannot be corrupted.

Outcomes and chances

Every result keeps the item and leaves it Corrupted. Ruined adds penalties; it does not destroy the item.
