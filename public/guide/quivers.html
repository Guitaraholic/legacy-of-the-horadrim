Quivers

Choose curses, speed, resistances or sockets for your bow or crossbow.

Arrows for bows. Bolts for crossbows. Every quiver is endless. Vendors sell white Hexed quivers. The other families drop or can be gambled. Hexed, Flight and Anointed quivers take the Horadric reagents like other gear.
