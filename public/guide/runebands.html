Runebands

The endgame chase, and the rarest rings in Sanctuary: each holds most of a famous runeword and waits for its final rune. Finish one and wear a runeword where no runeword fit before.

+

The Runeband of Enigma already holds Jah and Ith. Socket a Ber Rune and it becomes Enigma in a ring slot, leaving your body armour free for another runeword.
