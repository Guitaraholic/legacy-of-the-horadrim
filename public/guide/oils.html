Oil crafting

An oil craft makes a fresh item, so it destroys anything in its sockets (runes, gems, jewels). Remove what you want to keep before oiling. Signets and Pendants keep their socket.

Oils of the Sentinels add one guaranteed stat to an item in the Horadric Cube. The oil and the Perfect Gem pick the stat; the reagent picks the item's new quality.

Cube: Item + Reagent + Oil + Perfect Gem
Reagent:Quill of Inscription (normal → magic) · Jawbone of Lies (reroll magic) · Heart of Ascension (magic → rare) · Tail of Chaos (reroll rare)
