Remnants

What it does

Right-click a Remnant to gain a skill point. Each works once per character; skills take at most 25 hard points. Remnant sources: Andariel's Remnant: Echo of Andariel · Echo of Andariel (II) · Hell · 100%; Duriel's Remnant: Echo of Duriel · Echo of Duriel (III) · Hell · 100%; Mephisto's Remnant: Echo of Mephisto (I) · Hell · 100%; Diablo's Remnant: Echo of Diablo · Hell · 100%; Baal's Remnant: Echo of Baal · Hell · 100%. Cube any five Remnants for a Sealed Breath: each input gives a one-in-five chance of its boss’s Breath. Cube any five Sealed Breaths for a Fivefold Breath, chosen the same way.
