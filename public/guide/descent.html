The bands

Each band has ten floors and ends in an Echo arena. Follow the stairs to go deeper.

  1. Kill Baalon each difficulty
  2. The Worldstone Chambertake the stair in its north wall
  3. Arreat Descenttouch its waypoint; it brings you back
  4. Bands I–V10 floors each, stair to stair
  5. An echo below each bandin Hell, its sigil cubed in Harrogath opens it

After Baal, take the stairs between the two torches in the Worldstone Chamber's north wall. Touch the Arreat Descent waypoint; it replaces Arreat Plateau in Act V.

Floors have stairs up and down, but no waypoints. Echo arenas allow Town Portals. The final Echo ends the Descent.
