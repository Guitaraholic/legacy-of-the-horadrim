Magic and rare affixes

Choose an item type and base to compare its magic prefixes and suffixes. Filter by item level to plan a craft or reroll. The ranges below come from the current build.

Items can have only one affix per group. The quality filter removes affixes that cannot roll on rares. Built-in automatic bonuses are not part of this pool.

Item level is the level of your particular item. Affix level is calculated from item level, base quality level and any base magic level; it is not the required character level. An item-level range means the affix is eligible, not guaranteed. Rerolls can change item level; check the recipe.
