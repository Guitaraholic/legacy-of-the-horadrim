Bind a Journal

Leaf of Cain+2 unique small charms→Cain's Journal

The Leaf and both other charms are consumed. Carry one Journal at a time.

Choose your Journal

Target Leaves and Journals at the Gilded Wretch in Hell: 0.1196% (1 in 836) per named item. Leaves also drop elsewhere; expand a Leaf’s sources for every chance.

Compare each Journal’s bonuses and Cube ingredients. Carry one Journal at a time.
