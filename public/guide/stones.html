The run

  1. Cube five fragments in Harrogath.
  2. Enter the red portal.
  3. Hunt Ithmar the Waykeeper.
  4. Return by Town Portal.

Inside the Halls

  • Boss: Ithmar, at the end of the longest route.
  • Gems: normal / flawless / perfect, by difficulty.
  • Stone I: Ithmar the Waykeeper · Hell · 25%.

Ithmar’s rewards →

3Open a sealed place, then climb

Open

Sanctuary Stone IA stone+Any gem, any grade→A red portal

In Hell, cube a Sanctuary Stone with any grade of gem in Harrogath. The gem selects one of three destinations; both ingredients are consumed. Leave by Town Portal.
