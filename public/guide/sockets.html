These are type limits. A base can hold fewer sockets. Gloves and boots need item level 41+ to hold three.

Will it take the runeword?

  1. BaseNormal or superior, and one of the types listed by the runeword.
  2. SocketsExactly one for each rune. Empty sockets, no extras.
  3. OrderInsert the runes in the displayed order.
