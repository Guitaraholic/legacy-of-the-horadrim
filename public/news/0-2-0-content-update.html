Six stats that reshape skills

Guide

Legacy of the Horadrim 0.2.0 is the first content update to the public alpha of this offline mod for Diablo II: Resurrected and Reign of the Warlock. In 0.1.0, +# to Projectiles let projectile skills fire more. Now five more stats join it. Gear can widen a blast, stretch an aura or a curse, add hits to Chain Lightning and Lightning Strike, raise how many minions you keep, and make buffs and curses last longer. A sixth makes each extra projectile hit harder.

They roll on magic and rare items, come from Sentinel oils, and fill six new runewords: Flurry, Squall, Torrent, Cloudburst, Expanse and Mandate. Hail now comes later and fires more.

The Assassin and the Amazon are the first classes we tuned for them. Traps go up to seven. The Martial Arts charges last longer. An Amazon can keep a second Valkyrie.

Why new stats. In Diablo II, gear mostly makes a skill hit harder. We wanted gear that changes how a skill plays, so two builds of the same skill can feel different.

Soul of Legend

Soul of Legend is a new currency. Cube it with a plain weapon or armour, or a magic ring or amulet, and it becomes a unique for that slot. Your character level decides which uniques it can become. It never makes a boss's own reward.

Why a Soul. A good base you can't use should still be worth picking up. The Soul turns it into a unique worth a look.

Quivers that never run out

Guide

Arrows and bolts no longer run out. Four new quiver families drop instead: Hexed, Flight, Anointed and Graven. They curse what you shoot, speed your attacks, add resistances, or take sockets for gems and runes.

Why quivers. Running out of arrows mid-fight was never fun. A quiver is now gear worth finding.

New gear for the Assassin

Two three-piece sets drop from the Shades in the Stone maps: The Patient Hand for Martial Arts and The Second Shadow for Shadow Disciplines. Snarekeeper's Tread are trap boots, and Cinderwake is a fire-trap claw from the highest Stone tiers.

Why three pieces. Short sets leave room for uniques, so an Assassin can wear a set and still find a place for a favourite unique.

The Last Vigil

Guide

The Last Vigil now waits at the end of one straight crypt corridor, on an open pentagram. A player asked for it on our public tracker.

The Descent

Guide

The deeper floors of the Descent have new looks. The Realm of Destruction glows with ember and ash, and the Burrows of Pain hang with webs. Stairs on several floors now sit at opposite ends, so each floor is a walk.

We fixed the freeze in Cinders of Hatred Levels 2 and 3. If your game ever freezes, the report tool can now catch it.

What else changed

Gilded Fallen are tougher, more common, and now found in the Stone maps and the Descent. Keys stack to 50, and both tomes hold 50 scrolls. Every weapon, armor, jewel, charm, ring and amulet shows its item level. The currency guide now shows how each currency rolls.

It is still an alpha

Expect bugs, crashes and balance changes between releases. The crash reporter that comes with the mod sends us what went wrong, and the known issues page lists what we are already fixing. The 0.2.0 patch notes, linked below, list every change. Install Legacy of the Horadrim to begin.
