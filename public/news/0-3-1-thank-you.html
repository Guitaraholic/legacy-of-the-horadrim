Thank you for playing, and a small patch

2026-11-01

Thanks to everyone who has tried Legacy of the Horadrim so far. 0.3.1 fixes two freezes and tidies the loot filter; maps and balance come next.

Thank you to everyone who has downloaded Legacy of the Horadrim and given it a go. 0.3.0 only went out yesterday, and watching people actually play it has been the best part of the whole project. It means a lot that you're spending time with this alpha, and telling me where it needs work.

0.3.1 is a small patch. One of you reported that buying a Jewel from a gambler froze the game on "Waiting for confirmation of transaction". That's fixed. Talic froze on Arreat Summit whenever he used Whirlwind; he's back to being a normal problem. The loot filter also had a tidy: our items get tabs of their own, the Runebands tab lists all sixteen bands, and four quiver families have their own icons. The full patch notes are linked below.

What comes next

I'm going to give 0.3 a couple of weeks to settle while you play. After that, 0.4 is a refinement pass on map layouts across the endgame, the Sanctuary Stone maps and the Descent both, and 0.5 is aiming at a major balance pass.

Keep the bug reports coming, in the Discord's bug channel or on GitHub. They shaped this patch, and they'll shape the next one. Stay awhile, and listen.
