Jewellery with a socket

Guide

Legacy of the Horadrim 0.3.0 is the second content update to the public alpha of this offline mod for Diablo II: Resurrected and Reign of the Warlock. It puts sockets in jewellery. A Signet is a ring with one socket, and a Pendant is an amulet with one. A gem or rune in a Signet gives its weapon bonus. In a Pendant it gives its shield bonus.

Runebands are white rings that already hold all but the last rune of a runeword. Insert that last rune and the band becomes the whole runeword. Some unique Signets and Pendants grow as you feed them Perfect gems, up to ten.

Why sockets in jewellery. Rings and amulets were the one place a rune could never go. Now a spare rune can finish a ring.

A unique for every quiver

Guide

Each quiver family now has its own uniques, from The Quarreling Dead to The Bell of Bone. Quivers can be crafted, and the Horadric reagents remake them. Graven quivers now drop with one to three sockets and take five new runewords of their own.

Why quivers. In 0.2.0 a quiver became gear. Now it is gear worth chasing.

New rewards

Named bosses in Hell drop new uniques, among them Storm Brood and Gale Binder. The Fletcher's Watch is a new set for bow users. Three classes get a three-piece set and new uniques each. Six named Council members in Kurast drop a jewel of their own, a different one in each difficulty.

Gilded Fallen now run fast and glow gold. Each drops one large pile of gold, and its gear is rare or better.

Why. A Gilded Fallen should be a moment worth stopping for.

Every class gets a second look

Guide

The Barbarian, Amazon and Druid get stronger skills where they were weak. Whirlwind, Bash, Frenzy, Jab, Fend and the Druid's shapeshift skills hit harder or last longer. Three new stats join gear: extra attacks, faster pulses and tougher summons. The stats from 0.2.0 now reach many more skills.

Why. A build should be able to grow through its gear, not only its skill points.

Fixes

Deckard Cain now stands by the stash in Harrogath. The Eye of Reckoning keeps an ethereal item ethereal. Normal endgame areas drop loot for their level. The Echo of Mephisto shows his life bar. A shrine no longer sits inside a wall.

It is still an alpha

Expect bugs, crashes and balance changes between releases. The crash reporter that comes with the mod sends us what went wrong. The 0.3.0 patch notes, linked below, list every change. Install Legacy of the Horadrim to begin.
