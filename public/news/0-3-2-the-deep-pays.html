0.3.2: the deep pays, and bites back

2026-11-01

Endgame monsters drop three to five times the reagents and runes, the Descent grows more crowded, varied and dangerous with depth, and the Echoes finally hit like they should.

Thank you for all the reports on 0.3.1. You were clear about two things. The endgame asked a lot of you and didn't give enough back, and one trick gave back far too much. 0.3.2 is my answer to both.

What changes

The biggest change is to what you find. Monsters in the Stone maps and the Descent now drop reagents, Hellforge Embers and runes three to five times as often, and the deeper you go, the more they drop. Every kill should feel worth making.

Find Item goes back to being a nice extra. It still beats the kill itself, but a corpse no longer hands you a rare every time you search it.

The Descent also gets harder the further down you go. Each band is busier than the one above it, and every floor brings its own five monster families. From the Pits of Anguish down, monsters hit harder, and in the two deepest bands they move faster too. The Pits themselves have gone darker, with a cold jail and a violet gloom in the catacombs.

The Echoes now hit as hard as I always meant them to, from 83% of Uber Mephisto's blows up to 151% of Uber Baal's.

Last, the Options menu no longer crashes the game in the Burrows of Pain. To get there, I've stripped the lower spider caves back for now. The webs and cocoons will come back one at a time, as each one is proven safe.

Every number is in the patch notes below. Keep the reports coming, in the Discord's bug channel or on GitHub.
