0.0.868 · The first steps below

14 October 2026 · Retrospective highlights

The first ten floors of the Descent, six early Stone destinations, and equipment for the journey beyond Baal.

0.0.868 · Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with Earlier private builds; selected historical highlights, not a complete balance comparison.

New

The Descent begins beneath the Hellforge Breach, with ten floors leading to the Echo of Mephisto.

The first band opened in Normal and Nightmare for experience and gear; Hell held the Echo’s rewards. Later builds added four bands.

Current reference page: Descent →

Sanctuary Stones open six early side areas, with a separate lair beneath each map.

Destinations were Halls of Agony, Black Canyon Mines, Cisterns of Dahlgur, Caverns of Araneae, Ashwold Cemetery and Drowned Temples of Kurast, up to tier III.

Current reference page: Stones →
