0.0.2597 · Sentinels at the shrine

21 October 2026 · Retrospective highlights

Four Sentinel hunters bring exclusive rewards to the Stone climb, alongside Vault and equipment updates.

0.0.2597 · Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.2460.

NewOther fixesBalance

New

Barrow Sentinel, Tomb Sentinel, Rime Sentinel and Forge Sentinel join the Defiled Shrine encounters in Hell Stone tiers V–XV.

Current reference page: Sentinels →

The four Sentinels have exclusive uniques: Husk of Offerings, Ward of Four Shrines, The Shrine’s Due and The Altar’s Last Taper.

Current reference page: Sentinels →

  • Sentinel leaders can drop the Gilded Key.

Horadric Vault chests now provide a unique or rare item, perfect gems, a reagent and gold.

Current reference page: New Encounters →

Other fixes

Campaign Hell chests return to their original drops.

The Ossuary twins now use Oblivion Knight and Abyss Knight bodies.

River of Flame floor tiles now appear on the minimap.

The build planner now displays its controls on the first visit.

The website restores its subtle image backgrounds and improves its Stone drops and Terror Zone guides.
