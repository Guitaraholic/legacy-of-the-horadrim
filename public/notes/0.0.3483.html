0.0.3483 · Stone map crash test

23 October 2026 · Retrospective highlights

A crash test build for the Stone maps, and tougher Echo and Stone bosses.

0.0.3483 · Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3473.

NewOther fixesBalance

New

  • A new test copy of the Rubble of Viz-Jun, with its middle walls cleared, helps find the Stone map crash.

Other fixes

The Sunken Vaults of Valostre and the Undervaults of Kyovashad are smaller, to stop them crashing. Their bosses and Defiled Shrines are kept.

Balance

Hell Echo and Stone boss life and damage

The Hell Echoes are tougher: each now has more of its Uber's life and damage, from the Echo of Mephisto at about 548,000 life to about 997,000 for the Echo of Baal. Stone bosses follow the same curve up to tier XV. Tier I and II Stone bosses get the extra life only; their damage is unchanged.
