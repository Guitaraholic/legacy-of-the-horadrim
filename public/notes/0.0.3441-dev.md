# 0.0.3441 — The Pentagram of Chaos

The Shade of Diablo gets its own key, the Pentagram of Chaos, kept in the Relics tab.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3383.

## New

- The Pentagram of Chaos opens the Shade of Diablo&#x27;s arena when cubed with three Stones of Jordan. It drops from the Echo of Diablo and the tier XV Stone bosses in Hell, and has its own framed slot at the top of the Relics tab, where the Descent Sigil used to be.

## Balance

- The Echo of Diablo and the tier XV Stone bosses in Hell gain one drop roll for the Pentagram of Chaos, ahead of their loot.

### Echo of Diablo and tier XV Stone boss drops

| Stat | Condition | Before | After | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Drop rolls per kill | Hell Echo of Diablo | 8 rolls | 9 rolls; the new one is the Pentagram (1 in 20) | No existing drop removed |
| Drop rolls per kill | Hell tier XV Stone boss | 9 rolls | 10 rolls; the new one is the Pentagram (1 in 100) | No existing drop removed |

**Why:** Gives the Shade of Diablo&#x27;s new key a source, without removing any existing drop.
