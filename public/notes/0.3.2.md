# 0.3.2 — The deep pays, and bites back

Endgame monsters drop reagents, Embers and runes three to five times as often, Find Item no longer turns every corpse into a rare, the Descent&#x27;s deeper bands are more crowded, varied and dangerous, the Echo bosses hit as hard as intended, and a crash when opening Options in the Burrows of Pain is fixed.

Public · Compared with 0.3.1.

- [The Descent](#the-descent)

## The Descent

- Each Descent band has more monsters than the one above it: 15% more in the Pits of Anguish, rising to 60% more in the Realm of Destruction.
- Every Descent floor now has five monster families chosen to fit its look, and no two floors in a row share more than three. Suicide Minions no longer appear, and Stygian Dolls stay in a few pits.
- Descent monsters in Hell deal 10% more physical damage in the Pits of Anguish, rising to 50% more in the Realm of Destruction.
- Monsters in the Chasm of Terror move and attack 10% faster in Hell, and in the Realm of Destruction 15% faster.
- The five Echo bosses now hit at their intended share of their Uber&#x27;s damage: 83% for Mephisto, rising to 151% for Baal.
- Pits of Anguish Levels 4 to 10 are much darker, with a cold steel tint in the jail and a dark violet tint in the catacombs.

## Items and Crafting

- Monsters in Sanctuary Stone maps drop reagents and runes 3 times as often in tiers I to III, rising to 5 times in tiers XIII to XV.
- Monsters in the Descent drop reagents, Hellforge Embers and runes 3.25 to 4 times as often, depending on the band. With the extra monsters below, a full floor pays 3 times its old reagents in the Cinders of Hatred, rising to 5 times in the Realm of Destruction.
- Binding Blades, Runebands and other gear drop as before.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed a bug where using Find Item on a corpse in a Stone map or the Descent almost always found a rare item or a rune. Find Item now gives about 2.5 times the corpse&#x27;s own drop chances, and never a guarantee.
- Fixed a crash when opening the Options menu in the Burrows of Pain. For now, Levels 4 to 7 are plain caves without their green light, spider chests, cocoons and webs.
- Fixed a bug where Putrid Defilers in the Realm of Destruction and the Echo of the Worldstone were level 81 to 84 instead of their area&#x27;s level 109.
