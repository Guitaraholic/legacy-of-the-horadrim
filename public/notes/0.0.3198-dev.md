# 0.0.3198 — Map polish

Six maps get repairs: the Fire-Vents of Skovos, the Bastion Walls, the Hearthward Walls, the Fire-Roots of Arreat, the Menagerie of Fahir, the Moonwood and the Lost Gate of the Horadrim.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3022.

## Other fixes

- The Fire-Vents of Skovos boss now shows a boss marker.

- On the Bastion Walls, the cliff edges now run past the map corners, and the ice no longer overlaps the middle lake.

- The top-right corners of the Hearthward Walls and the Fire-Roots of Arreat now close properly.

- In the Menagerie of Fahir, the empty corner now has monsters.

- In the Moonwood, the gap in the wall where the river meets the map edge is closed.

- In the Lost Gate of the Horadrim, the tombs facing the lower edge now draw correctly.
