# 0.0.4030 — Binding Blades, finished

Binding Blades gain perfect versions, extra projectiles and Censure&#x27;s own bolt, and drop from Stone map monsters too; four blades and a runeword are renamed, Hail&#x27;s tooltip reads cleanly, and Burrows of Pain gets its spider cocoons.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3983.

## New

- Each school&#x27;s final-tier Binding Blade has a rare perfect version with all four spells at level 25, found only by rerolling a final-tier blade with the Eye of Reckoning (1 in 100 for each school&#x27;s perfect version).

- Second-tier Binding Blades carry +1 to Projectiles and final-tier blades +1 to +2 (every school but Blight), so their spells fan out. Censure&#x27;s blades now cast Bolt of Censure on attack: a holy bolt that strikes every enemy and fans with extra projectiles.

- First-tier Binding Blades now also drop from monsters in the Sanctuary Stone maps in Hell, at the same rate as in the Descent.

- The wiki gains a Binding Blades guide with every school, tier and reroll chance, and the build planner gains a magic charm creator.

## Other fixes

- Four Binding Blades are renamed so they no longer share a name with other items: Frostbite is now Coldsnap, Heaven&#x27;s Wrath is Skyfall, Judgement is Tribunal and False Promise is Silver Tongue. The runeword Judgment is now spelled Judgement.

- +# to Projectiles now reads &quot;+1 to Projectiles&quot; on Hail, Deluge and the blades, instead of &quot;+1 +%d to Projectiles&quot;.

- Burrows of Pain Levels 4 to 7 now have the Spider Cavern&#x27;s breakable cocoons to find, and the webbed bodies that floated in the air now lie on the floor.
