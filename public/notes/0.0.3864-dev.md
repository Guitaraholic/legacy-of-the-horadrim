# 0.0.3864 — Pits, Rubble and Barrows tidied

The Fighting Pits become one flat arena inside Travincal&#x27;s wall, Rubble of Viz-Jun keeps its edges, the Barrows lose their brown squares, and many uniques and set items show their own art.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3848.

## New

- The Corrupted line on an item is shown in red.

## Other fixes

- The Fighting Pits of Old Kehjan are one flat floor inside Travincal&#x27;s city wall, with an arena of braziers and torch posts around Kharazad the Thrice-Slain. No invisible edge, no floating props.

- Rubble of Viz-Jun keeps its trees and walls along every edge, the top edge follows a river bank, and a few ruined blocks break up the grid.

- The Barrows of the Old Kings have grass ground instead of brown squares, no invisible walls around the mausoleums, and a clear path to Osgar the Barrow-King.

- Over two hundred uniques and set items now show their own inventory art instead of their base item&#x27;s.
