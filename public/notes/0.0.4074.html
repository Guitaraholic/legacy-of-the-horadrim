0.0.4074 · Blades that spin spells

26 October 2026 · Retrospective highlights

Each Binding Blade now grants its school's own Whirlwind, which casts the school's spell mid-spin, and the climb to the top tier costs dearer runes.

0.0.4074 · Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.4068.

NewBalance

New

Every Binding Blade grants +1 to its school's Whirlwind in place of +1 to Whirlwind, on any class. While you spin it casts the school's strike spell: Fire Ball, Frost Nova, Lightning, Poison Nova, Bone Spear, Tornado, Blessed Hammer, Miasma Bolt or Fire Blast. Spells on normal attacks are unchanged.

Chance per spin hit and spell level: Bound 20% at level 14, Warded 25% at level 18, Sealed 30% at level 22, Perfect 30% at level 25. +skills don't raise them. The spin hits as hard as Whirlwind: a Barbarian's spin uses their own Whirlwind level plus one.
