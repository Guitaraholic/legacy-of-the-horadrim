# 0.0.3692 — Sentinel gear

Mercenary Sentinel uniques, Sentinel-rune runewords, and Smite no longer stun-locks the Crypt bosses.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3656.

## New

- 21 new mercenary uniques (rings, amulets, belts, gloves and boots) drop from the Hell map Sentinels and their shades.

- 47 new runewords use the Sentinel runes Myr, Xeth and Vot.

## Other fixes

- The Crypt of the Vigil&#x27;s shades and the Last Vigil are no longer knocked back or stun-locked by Smite.
