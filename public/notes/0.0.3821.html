0.0.3821 · Maps and corruption

24 October 2026 · Retrospective highlights

The Fighting Pits, Rubble of Viz-Jun and the Barrows rebuilt, the Claw of Prising fixed, and corruption made permanent.

0.0.3821 · Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3707.

NewOther fixes

New

  • A corrupted item is permanent: socketing, rerolling and unsocketing recipes refuse it, and repairs still work.

Other fixes

The Fighting Pits of Old Kehjan and the Rubble of Viz-Jun have closed, even edges and walls.

The Barrows are rebuilt as a cemetery with huts and mausoleums, and Osgar the Barrow-King no longer gets stuck.

The Claw of Prising destroys the item and gives back a Gutted Scrap and every rune or gem in it, the same on every item.

Map 144 is now the Sunken Jungle of Kurast.
