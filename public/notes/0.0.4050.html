0.0.4050 · Fuller Descent, clearer blades

25 October 2026 · Retrospective highlights

The Descent fills up halfway to the Stone maps, Binding Blades name the runes that climb them, and the wiki puts +# to Projectiles up front.

0.0.4050 · Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.4038.

NewOther fixesBalance

New

  • Binding Blades now show the runes that raise them to the next tier; the top tier reads "Fully awakened".
  • The wiki's Overview and home page now present +# to Projectiles: what it does, where it comes from, and the 35 skills it drives.

Other fixes

Cinders of Hatred Level 8's stairs up and down now sit at opposite ends of the floor.

The wiki's searchable pickers filter again, searching a group's name such as Binding Blades finds its items, and the footer links a site map of every page.

Balance

Descent density

Descent floors now hold more monsters and larger packs, halfway between their old density and the Stone maps of the same band, on every difficulty.

Why: The Descent felt light next to the Stone maps.
