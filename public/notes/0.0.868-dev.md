# 0.0.868 — The first steps below

The first ten floors of the Descent, six early Stone destinations, and equipment for the journey beyond Baal.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with Earlier private builds; selected historical highlights, not a complete balance comparison.

## New

- The Descent begins beneath the Hellforge Breach, with ten floors leading to the Echo of Mephisto.

- Sanctuary Stones open six early side areas, with a separate lair beneath each map.

- Early equipment gives new characters more choices before the endgame.

- Charsi’s Cinch joins the unique equipment collection.

- Alkor’s Transmutation adds a unique jewel to hunt.

- Namegiver offers another unique weapon to find.

- Runewords broaden the equipment choices from the campaign onward.

- Daylight is part of the early runeword collection.

- Darkness is another option in that collection.

- Candle is one of the collection’s shorter words.
