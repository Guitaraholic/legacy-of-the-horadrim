# 0.0.3532 — Convergence uniques

Four new uniques grow from seeds dropped in the Convergence, fed with Remnants and Last Breaths.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3521.

## New

- Four new uniques grow from seeds found in the Convergence: The Black Bloom, Mark of Ruin, The Restless Dead and The Blood Wage. Each seed is fed twice in the Horadric Cube, with Remnants and then a Last Breath, and the Remnants you choose set which version you get.
