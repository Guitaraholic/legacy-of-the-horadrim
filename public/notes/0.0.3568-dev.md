# 0.0.3568 — The Claw of Prising and the Last Vigil

The Claw of Prising now breaks the item into Gutted Scrap, and the Last Vigil is bigger and faster.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3548.

## Other fixes

- The Claw of Prising now destroys the item it&#x27;s used on, turning it into Gutted Scrap, and gives back every rune, gem and jewel from its sockets.

- The Last Vigil is twice its old size and moves faster, and still falls when it dies.
