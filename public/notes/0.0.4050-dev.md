# 0.0.4050 — Fuller Descent, clearer blades

The Descent fills up halfway to the Stone maps, Binding Blades name the runes that climb them, and the wiki puts +# to Projectiles up front.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.4038.

## New

- Binding Blades now show the runes that raise them to the next tier; the top tier reads &quot;Fully awakened&quot;.

- The wiki&#x27;s Overview and home page now present +# to Projectiles: what it does, where it comes from, and the 35 skills it drives.

## Other fixes

- Cinders of Hatred Level 8&#x27;s stairs up and down now sit at opposite ends of the floor.

- The wiki&#x27;s searchable pickers filter again, searching a group&#x27;s name such as Binding Blades finds its items, and the footer links a site map of every page.

## Balance

- Descent floors now hold more monsters and larger packs, halfway between their old density and the Stone maps of the same band, on every difficulty.

### Descent density

| Stat | Condition | Before | After | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monster density and champion and unique packs | Every Descent floor, every difficulty | Set by the floor&#x27;s pressure tier | Halfway from that to the average of its band&#x27;s Stone maps | More monsters and packs on every floor |

**Why:** The Descent felt light next to the Stone maps.
