# 0.0.3265 — Map and boss repairs

The Canyons of Dahlgur no longer trap you at the spawn, two bosses and the Last Vigil get proper bodies, and four maps are repaired.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3222.

## New

- Each Canticle now says: Transmute with a Perfect Gem to sing another verse.

## Other fixes

- In the Canyons of Dahlgur, you can walk out of the spawn corner in every direction.

- Kyrene in the Fire-Vents of Skovos and Sahra in the Moonless Sands now walk, attack, can be targeted and show their boss marker.

- The Last Vigil now takes the form of Lister the Tormentor and falls when killed. Ghorvax in the Fire-Roots of Arreat is now a Frozen Horror.

- On the Bastion Walls, the ice cliff now runs past the bottom-right corner.

- The top-right corners of the Hearthward Walls and the Fire-Roots of Arreat now show ground, not black.

- In the Menagerie of Fahir, nine crates now cover the old trapdoor pit.
