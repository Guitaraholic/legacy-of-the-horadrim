# 0.0.3656 — Signature loot

Each Stone map&#x27;s last boss can drop a themed extra in Hell.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3630.

## New

- In Hell, each Stone map&#x27;s last boss can drop one extra item that fits its map, on top of its usual loot.

## Other fixes

- The Crypt of the Vigil&#x27;s shades and the Last Vigil can no longer be stun-locked.

- The Fighting Pits of Old Kehjan&#x27;s north yard has its city wall back, without the half buildings at the sides.
