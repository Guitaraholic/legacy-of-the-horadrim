# 0.0.4095 — The 0.1.0 release candidate

Pits of Anguish is rebuilt to end its crash, more Descent floors have their stairs far apart, top-tier Binding Blades roll their spell level, and uniques never turn rare when rerolled.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.4074.

## New

- Sealed Binding Blades now come at spell level 20, 22 or 24, with every spell on the blade and its Whirlwind moving together. Seal boss drops pick evenly between them, and the Eye of Reckoning rerolls the level as well as the school. A Perfect blade, with everything at level 25, is 1 in 100 per Eye.

## Other fixes

- Pits of Anguish Levels 1 to 3 are rebuilt smaller, at 12 rooms each, ending the crash on Level 2.

- Burrows of Pain Levels 5 and 7 and Chasm of Terror Level 4 now have their stairs up and down at opposite ends of the floor.

- Rerolling a unique or set item with the Eye of Reckoning never turns it into a rare. Unique and set items can now drop more than once in the same game.
