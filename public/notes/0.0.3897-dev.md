# 0.0.3897 — Bosses fall, edges thinned

Osgar and three other Stone bosses now fight and die properly, Rubble&#x27;s edges are thinned, the Fighting Pits show their moat, and rings and amulets can be corrupted.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3864.

## New

- Rings and amulets can now be corrupted with a Hellforge Ember, with their own table of outcomes. A corrupted ring or amulet is refused by every recipe that would change it.

## Other fixes

- Osgar the Barrow-King no longer freezes mid-swing: he dies, falls and drops his loot. He now waits in the open middle of the Barrows of the Old Kings. Vhassun, Inquisitor Eldric Vosse and Mordrick had the same fault and are fixed too.

- Rubble of Viz-Jun keeps its river bank and closed edges with far fewer trees along them, and fewer loose props through the middle.

- Fighting Pits of Old Kehjan: the floor no longer covers the moat outside the city wall, so it should draw as water; the west wall stops you at its face like the others, and the open middle has braziers, Kurast idols, baskets and the dead.

- The wiki&#x27;s drop information is cut back to what matters, the home page shows every new feature in turn, and oils and wax runes have their own pages.
