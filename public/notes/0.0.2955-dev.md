# 0.0.2955 — Visible shrines, full uniques and a tougher Last Vigil

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.2931.

## Other fixes

- The Horadric Vault chests, the Defiled Shrine and the Portal Shrine are now visible.

- Sentinels&#x27; Votive and The Unbroken Oath now show their inventory art.

- Oils now say in their tooltip that any three can be transmuted for a random oil.

- In the Menagerie of Fahir, a sewer grate now covers the hole the old trapdoor left in the north corner.

- The Last Vigil now fights in a larger hall with champion packs, moves much faster and shows its boss marker.

- In the Canyons of Dahlgur you walk on the ground you see, and Sadiq can be reached down the east side.

- Crash reports now wait for the crash file and include the game state at the moment of the crash.
