# 0.0.2899 — The Vigil and crash retests

A new Sentinel gauntlet and reward chain arrive alongside targeted crash diagnostics, map repairs and stronger release checks.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.2632.

## New

- Four Sentinel seal quarters now open the Crypt of the Vigil in Hell Harrogath. Fight four Sentinel shades, then The Last Vigil. The guardian can drop six exclusive uniques: The Unbowed, The Unbroken Oath, The Vigil Girdle, Stonefast, Hands of Mercy and Sentinels’ Votive.

- Sentinel leaders now supply six oils and their matching seal quarter. Three oils can be exchanged for one oil. Xeth, Vot and Myr runes join the Sentinel reward chain, with dedicated storage and artwork.

- The Silver City’s three lieutenants use larger Izual models only in this area.

- Choir of the Silver City can now drop from the Silver City’s host in Hell, at about one drop per 40 kills. This level-90 ring grants level 10–15 Conviction, 10–15 Magic Damage Reduced, 5–8 Replenish Life and +2 Light Radius. Conviction does not stack with another Conviction aura; the higher level wins.

## Other fixes

- A Gilded Key alone opens the permanent Gilded Vault in Hell Harrogath. The Vault keeps its 25 Gilded Fallen and clean floor. Its stair covers are 20% larger than in the previous build.

- Festering Woods gains a denser canopy with solid tree trunks and clear routes. Hearthward Walls and Fire-Roots of Arreat remove the flat background terrain that cut across their cliff corners. Hearthward’s snowy rocks sit on native ground.

- The Barrows’ burial courts regain their grave dressing and lose excess chests and the misplaced cliff. Dahlgur’s raised tomb and cliff artwork sits at floor height.

- Placed objects now receive their client model mappings, addressing invisible objects such as the Vault chests. The Portal Shrine in the Shrine Warden room moves into view. These changes still need in-game retests.

- The automap returns to the original game’s rows and lookup blocks. Custom minimap replacements are removed while the town, corridor and traversal crashes are retested. This is not a claim that those crashes are solved.

- The crash collector can submit a private report or produce text for manual reporting. Installation and release checks strengthen save-row, recipe, artwork and item-source validation. Mod packaging and website publication run as separate steps.

- Known issue: Steam Deck and Linux crash-report uploads are blocked by Cloudflare error 1010 in this build. Collect and retain dumps locally. Windows PowerShell upload works.

## Balance

- Shrine Sentinel leaders draw at 1.5 times their native body size.

### The four shrine Sentinel leaders

| Stat | Condition | Before | After | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Visible body size | Barrow, Tomb, Rime and Forge shrine leaders | 1× native body | 1.5× native body | 50% larger drawing; no extra damage implied. |

**Why:** Make shrine leaders easier to recognize and separate from escorts.

- The Silver City has denser packs, larger bodies and a stronger host.
