# 0.0.3383 — Crash layouts, Golden Oil classes and the Claw

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3265.

## Other fixes

- The Haunted Slums, the Shadowed Streets of Ureh and the Undervaults of Ivgorod now use new layouts, to stop their crashes.

- Golden Oil now gives each class item its own class&#x27;s skills. Shared items, such as daggers, roll one of the classes that use them at even odds, and the oil&#x27;s tooltip says so.

- The Claw of Prising returns every gem, rune and jewel from a socketed item, plus Gutted Scrap.

- The Bastion Walls&#x27; bottom-right corner and the top-left corners of the Hearthward Walls and the Fire-Roots of Arreat no longer show black.

## Balance

- Sanctuary Stone map bosses from tier IV to XV now always roll a Perfect Gem drop on top of their loot.

### Stone map boss drops, tiers IV–XV

| Stat | Condition | Before | After | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Drop rolls per boss kill | Hell, tier IV–XV Stone boss | 6 rolls | 7 rolls, one of them a Perfect Gem | One extra Perfect Gem roll per kill |

**Why:** Endgame sustain: Perfect Gems feed oil crafting, and the gem roll comes before the loot so the item cap trims loot first.

- The Echo of Mephisto gains an extra Stone roll, and its Stone rolls now come before its loot, so the item cap trims loot first.

### Echo of Mephisto drops

| Stat | Condition | Before | After | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Drop rolls per kill | Echo of Mephisto | 7 rolls | 8 rolls, with an extra Stone roll ahead of the loot | One more Stone roll; Stone rolls are never trimmed by the item cap |

**Why:** Puts the Echo&#x27;s Stone rolls ahead of its loot so the item cap never trims them.
