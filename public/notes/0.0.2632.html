0.0.2632 · Map edges and vault repairs

21 October 2026 · Retrospective highlights

This private alpha update repairs map edges, visible objects, storage frames and amulet recipes.

0.0.2632 · Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.2597.

NewOther fixes

New

  • Named amulets have distinct approved artwork, including the Canticle variants.

Other fixes

Relics storage groups the Vault Sigil and Gilded Key above the four Sentinel materials on desktop and controller. The Stones tab no longer shows an unused sixth slot frame.

Defiled Shrines and Horadric Vault chests have their missing client graphics entries restored.

Terrain collision is clipped to each map piece’s bounds to address the Kurast edge crashes.

Sealed Breaths have their own loot-filter category. Boss organs, sigils, seals and the Gilded Key appear under Uber Materials; Sanctuary Stones contains only Stones.

The snowy watch-fire map has rocks closing the open top corners.

The Horadric Vault is brighter and has loose blood effects removed.

Canticle aura swaps can be repeated in the same game without becoming a rare amulet.

Broken Barricades replaces the eastern tall ice wall with a sunken ravine. The central wooden huts are removed and their ground is filled with snow.

Moonwood removes blocking bank trees, bushes, rocks and walls so the river can be approached. The river extends beyond the south edge.

Wortham replaces the misplaced dark cliff with grass. The cave mouth that belonged to that cliff piece is also removed.

The website improves navigation and readability.
