0.0.1448 · Stones and deeper roads

16 October 2026 · Retrospective highlights

Sanctuary Stones reach tier XV, while the Descent grows beyond its first Echo.

0.0.1448 · Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.868.

New

The Stone climb now reaches tier XV, with thirty-six additional places, their bosses and curses.

These maps used simpler room layouts and boss lairs.

Current reference page: Stones →

Four more ten-floor bands carry the Descent down to the Echo of Baal.

In Hell, the Echoes provide the sigils for the next bands. This build also records the restored route from the Hellforge Breach into the Descent.

Current reference page: Descent →

Hell act bosses can drop Sanctuary Stone I on repeat kills, with a one-in-four chance.

This gives characters a route into Stone maps without relying only on Terror Zones.

Runewords support distinct equipment and companion choices.

Examples include Cortege of Bones for companions, Fastness and Glory; these are not their first-release dates.

Cortege of Bones is one of the companion runewords.
