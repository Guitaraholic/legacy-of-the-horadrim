# 0.0.3955 — More projectiles

A new item stat, +# to Projectiles, makes projectile skills fire extra missiles, carried by two new runewords, Hail and Deluge; the 0.1.0 news article reads more cleanly.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3944.

## New

- A new item stat, +# to Projectiles, makes projectile skills fire extra missiles. Fire Ball, Bone Spear, Tornado and the other one-bolt skills fan out, and each extra projectile raises the volley&#x27;s total damage by 35%, shared across the fan. Multiple Shot, Strafe and the other many-missile skills fire more missiles at full damage. Without the stat, every skill fires exactly what it does today. Two new three-rune runewords carry it: Hail (Tal Ort Sol, helms, level 27) and Deluge (Lem Ist Gul, helms and gloves, level 53).

## Other fixes

- The 0.1.0 news article on the wiki reads more cleanly.
