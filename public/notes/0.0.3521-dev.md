# 0.0.3521 — Remnants and the Breath ladder

Spare echo elixirs become Remnants that cube up into Sealed and Fivefold Breaths, with room for them in the stash.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3483.

## New

- Spare echo elixirs are now Remnants. Cube five Remnants into a Sealed Breath, and five Sealed Breaths into a Fivefold Breath. The mix you put in sets the odds of which boss&#x27;s Breath comes out.

- The Relics, Stones and Runecraft stash tabs have new slots for Remnants and Sealed Breaths, and some items have moved to the tab that fits them.

## Other fixes

- Each Sentinel leader now drops its own Seal Quarter.

- Every Defiled Shrine on a Stone map now stands in the open, not behind a wall.

- The Canyons of Dahlgur are rebuilt as a cliff-walled desert basin, with no floating ground or invisible walls.

- The upper-left edges of the Hearthward Walls and the Fire-Roots of Arreat now show real ground instead of black.
