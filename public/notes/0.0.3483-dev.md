# 0.0.3483 — Stone map crash test

A crash test build for the Stone maps, and tougher Echo and Stone bosses.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3473.

## New

- A new test copy of the Rubble of Viz-Jun, with its middle walls cleared, helps find the Stone map crash.

## Other fixes

- The Sunken Vaults of Valostre and the Undervaults of Kyovashad are smaller, to stop them crashing. Their bosses and Defiled Shrines are kept.

## Balance

- The Hell Echoes are tougher: each now has more of its Uber&#x27;s life and damage, from the Echo of Mephisto at about 548,000 life to about 997,000 for the Echo of Baal. Stone bosses follow the same curve up to tier XV. Tier I and II Stone bosses get the extra life only; their damage is unchanged.

### Hell Echo and Stone boss life and damage

| Stat | Condition | Before | After | Impact |

| Share of its Uber&#x27;s life and damage | Hell Echo of Diablo | 110% | 134% | Tougher |
| Share of its Uber&#x27;s life and damage | Hell Echo of Baal | 125% | 151% | Tougher |

**Why:** The first Echo was weaker than Hell Baal; each Echo should be a clear step up from the one before.
