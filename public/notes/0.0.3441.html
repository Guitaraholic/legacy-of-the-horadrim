0.0.3441 · The Pentagram of Chaos

23 October 2026 · Retrospective highlights

The Shade of Diablo gets its own key, the Pentagram of Chaos, kept in the Relics tab.

0.0.3441 · Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3383.

NewBalance

New

  • The Pentagram of Chaos opens the Shade of Diablo's arena when cubed with three Stones of Jordan. It drops from the Echo of Diablo and the tier XV Stone bosses in Hell, and has its own framed slot at the top of the Relics tab, where the Descent Sigil used to be.

Balance

Echo of Diablo and tier XV Stone boss drops

The Echo of Diablo and the tier XV Stone bosses in Hell gain one drop roll for the Pentagram of Chaos, ahead of their loot.
