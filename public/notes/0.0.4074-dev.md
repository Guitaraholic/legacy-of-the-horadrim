# 0.0.4074 — Blades that spin spells

Each Binding Blade now grants its school&#x27;s own Whirlwind, which casts the school&#x27;s spell mid-spin, and the climb to the top tier costs dearer runes.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.4068.

## New

- Every Binding Blade grants +1 to its school&#x27;s Whirlwind in place of +1 to Whirlwind, on any class. While you spin it casts the school&#x27;s strike spell: Fire Ball, Frost Nova, Lightning, Poison Nova, Bone Spear, Tornado, Blessed Hammer, Miasma Bolt or Fire Blast. Spells on normal attacks are unchanged.

## Balance

- Raising a Binding Blade now costs dearer runes.

### Binding Blades: the rune climb

| Stat | Condition | Before | After | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Runes to raise a Bound blade to Warded | Horadric Cube, same school | Lem + Pul + Um | Mal + Ist + Gul | Three steps higher on the rune ladder |
| Runes to raise a Warded blade to Sealed | Horadric Cube, same school | Mal + Ist + Gul | Vex + Ohm + Lo | Three steps higher on the rune ladder |

**Why:** With spells cast mid-spin, the top tier is much stronger and should be rare.

- Seal bosses drop a Sealed Binding Blade less often.

### Binding Blades: Sealed blade drops

| Stat | Condition | Before | After | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chance of a Sealed blade | Each Seal boss kill (the Bonebound Twins: per fight) | 1 in 40 | 1 in 100 | 2.5 times rarer |

**Why:** With spells cast mid-spin, the top tier is much stronger and should be rare.
