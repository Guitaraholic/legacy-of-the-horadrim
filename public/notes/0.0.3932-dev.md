# 0.0.3932 — Stairs you can reach

Four Descent floors and Ashwold Cemetery no longer trap you away from a stair or let you walk off the map, Osgar swings at you, and every LotH ring and amulet takes a Hellforge Ember.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3897.

## Other fixes

- Descent floors 208, 209 and 210 (the Spider-cave band) and Cinders of Hatred floor 191 (the Durance of Hate) could split so a stair sat in a part of the floor you couldn&#x27;t walk to. One map piece on each is swapped for its other vanilla version, so both stairs can be reached from where you arrive. The stairs themselves haven&#x27;t moved.

- Ashwold Cemetery: the north-west fence corner no longer lets you walk out to the edge of the map.

- Osgar the Barrow-King now fights as a Doom Knight should: he swings at you instead of only throwing sparks. His loot and damage are unchanged.

- Every LotH ring and amulet that can be corrupted now takes a Hellforge Ember, including the Sentinel rings and amulets on their own bases.
