# 0.0.3630 — Map moods and boss pictures

Moods and weather on the endgame maps, the pinnacle short list, new boss pictures, and fixes to the Rubble and the Fighting Pits.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3580.

## New

- Endgame maps get their own mood: new tints in the Halls of Agony, the Archives of Zoltun Kulle and the Fires of Tristram; fog in the Ashwold Cemetery; drips, rain and mud in the Drowned Temples; wind in the Moonwood; and lava glow with embers in the Fire-Vents and the first Echo.

- The Avatar of Belial, the Shade of Bartuc and the Echo of the Veiled Prophet have their own pictures on the wiki.

## Other fixes

- The Rubble of Viz-Jun&#x27;s west wall is solid, and an invisible wall stops you at the edge of the Fighting Pits&#x27; south bridge.

- The Last Vigil is visible again, larger than the shades it leads, and walks faster.
