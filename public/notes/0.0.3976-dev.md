# 0.0.3976 — Binding Blades

Binding Blades arrive: 27 spell-casting blades in nine schools that climb with runes and reroll their school with the Eye of Reckoning, and the Descent&#x27;s Nightmare and Hell monsters now drop the Descent&#x27;s own loot.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3955.

## New

- Binding Blades: 27 new uniques on the Falchion, nine schools of magic (Ember, Rime, Tempest, Blight, Marrow, Gale, Censure, Malison and Guile) in three tiers. Every blade grants +1 to Whirlwind, so any class can Whirlwind with it, and is Indestructible; fast hits cast the school&#x27;s spells on striking and on kills, the second tier adds a spell on attack and the final tier one when struck. Lem + Pul + Um climbs a blade to the second tier and Mal + Ist + Gul to the final one, keeping its school; the Eye of Reckoning rerolls a blade into any school of the same tier with fresh rolls. First-tier blades drop from Descent monsters, second-tier from Stone bosses and the final tier from the Seal bosses. Their numbers are still being tuned.

## Other fixes

- Descent monsters in Nightmare and Hell now drop the Descent&#x27;s own loot, including Hellforge Embers, which they never did before; champions and unique packs keep their own better drops.
