# 0.0.2918 — Arreat&#x27;s Defiance and crash fixes

A new Barbarian blade joins the Seal I chase, and a map crash, a canyon&#x27;s collision and crash reporting are repaired.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.2899.

## New

- Arreat&#x27;s Defiance, a unique Phase Blade with +3-5 to Whirlwind (Barbarian Only) and -10% to Enemy Physical Resistance, can drop from the Avatar of Azmodan.

## Other fixes

- The Menagerie of Fahir no longer crashes the game while you walk its edges.

- In the Canyons of Dahlgur, tombs and props now block movement and sit on the ground.

- Crash reports now send from the Steam Deck, and Windows shows the server&#x27;s message when a report fails.

- The Sentinel guide shows the Crypt of the Vigil&#x27;s boss on its own page.
