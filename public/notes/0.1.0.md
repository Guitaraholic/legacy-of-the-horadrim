# 0.1.0 — The road beyond Baal

The first public alpha of Legacy of the Horadrim: the whole campaign with new loot from Normal onward, then a full endgame after Baal: the 50-floor Descent, 45 Sanctuary Stone maps, the Sentinel Vigil, Heaven, the Convergence and the Shade of Diablo. Expect bugs and balance changes.

Public · Compared with Diablo II: Resurrected (Reign of the Warlock, patch 3.3), unmodded.

## The Descent

- Added stairs in the Worldstone Chamber that lead to Arreat Descent after Baal&#x27;s defeat.
- Added 50 Descent floors beneath Arreat Descent, available on every difficulty.
- The five bands contain ten floors each, in order: Cinders of Hatred, Pits of Anguish, Burrows of Pain, Chasm of Terror and Realm of Destruction.
- Each band uses dungeons from one Evil&#x27;s domain, including Kurast&#x27;s sewers, the Durance of Hate, the Maggot Lair and the River of Flame.
- Descent area levels range from 30 to 45 in Normal.
- Descent area levels range from 64 to 68 in Nightmare.
- Hell Descent monster levels rise floor by floor, from 92 at the start of band I to 109 at the end of band V.
- Descent monsters in Nightmare and Hell drop the Descent&#x27;s own loot, including Hellforge Embers and new uniques; champions and unique packs keep their own better drops.
- Added an Echo arena at the end of each band, in order: Mephisto, Andariel, Duriel, Diablo and Baal.
- Each successive Echo is stronger than the last.
- The Echo of Mephisto has 547,791 life in Hell, 83% of Uber Mephisto&#x27;s life.
- The Echo of Baal has 996,675 life in Hell, 1.5 times a vanilla uber&#x27;s life.
- Each Hell Echo always drops its Remnant.
- Right-clicking a Remnant grants one skill point, once per Evil per character, for five additional skill points in total.
- Each Hell Echo has an 85% chance to drop its trophy.
- Hell Echoes also drop Sanctuary Stones and their own uniques.
- Each Hell Echo has a 5% chance to drop its Sigil, which reopens its arena without the Descent walk.
- In Normal and Nightmare, each Echo drops the loot of the Evil it echoes and a rune.
- Spare Remnants can advance the Convergence&#x27;s growing uniques.
- Cubing five Remnants produces a Sealed Breath, with each Remnant giving a one-in-five chance of its Evil&#x27;s Breath.
- El-to-Zod runes drop 1.5 times as often from Descent band II and twice as often from band IV.

## Sanctuary Stones

- Added 45 Sanctuary Stone maps across fifteen tiers, with three destinations per tier.
- In Harrogath on Hell, cubing a Stone with any gem opens a destination from that Stone&#x27;s tier.
- The gem determines which of the tier&#x27;s three destinations opens.
- Each destination has its own map and named boss.
- Added Stone bosses including Haelric the Jailer in the Yards of Agony, Queen Araneae, King Fahir in his Menagerie and Osgar the Barrow-King.
- Monster levels rise from 92 at tier I to 109 at tier XV.
- Stone boss life rises from 253,907 to 996,675, with the latter equal to 1.5 times a vanilla uber&#x27;s life.
- Cubing three Stones of one tier produces one Stone of the next tier.
- Sanctuary Stones drop from Hell monsters, Stone bosses and the Echoes.
- El-to-Zod runes drop 1.5 times as often from tier VI and twice as often from tier XI.

## The Fractured Halls

- Added Stone Fragments to act boss drops.
- Andariel always drops her Stone Fragment.
- Duriel, Mephisto, Diablo and Baal each have a one-in-four chance to drop their Fragment in Normal, rising to one in two in Hell.
- In Harrogath on any difficulty, cubing all five Stone Fragments opens the Fractured Halls.
- The Fractured Halls use the cracked halls of the Worldstone Keep.
- Added Ithmar the Waykeeper in the deepest room of the Fractured Halls.
- Ithmar drops a gem on every difficulty.
- In Hell, Ithmar has a one-in-four chance to drop a Sanctuary Stone.

## Sentinels and the Crypt of the Vigil

- Added four Sentinels: Barrow, Tomb, Rime and Forge.
- Sentinels guard Defiled Shrines on Hell Stone maps from tier V to tier XV.
- Each Sentinel leader drops its own seal quarter and a shrine oil.
- Sentinel leaders can also drop an exclusive unique.
- Sentinel champions have a one-in-six chance to drop the Gilded Key.
- Sentinel champions, leaders and shades have a one-in-six chance to drop one of 21 new rings, amulets, belts, gloves and boots.
- In Harrogath on Hell, cubing one seal quarter of each kind opens the Crypt of the Vigil.
- Added four Sentinel shades to the Crypt, with life ranging from 750,000 to 1.1 million.
- Three Sentinel shades have an elemental immunity.
- Added the Last Vigil, an Extra Fast Frozen Horror with 2 million life, cold breath and Holy Freeze.
- Champion packs guard the Last Vigil.
- The Last Vigil drops Sentinels&#x27; Votive, The Unbowed and The Unbroken Oath.
- The Sentinel shades and the Last Vigil drop the three new wax runes.

## Seal Ubers and Heaven

- Hell Stone bosses on tiers XIII to XV can drop five Seals, each with a one-in-25 drop chance.
- Cubing a Seal alone opens its uber encounter.
- Added the Avatar of Azmodan in the Court of Sin.
- Added the Avatar of Belial in the Hall of Lies.
- Added the Bonebound Twins in the Ossuary.
- Added the Shade of Bartuc on the Throne of the Warlord.
- Added the Shade of Zoltun Kulle behind Kulle&#x27;s Last Door.
- Each Seal uber has 1 million life.
- Each Seal uber drops its trophy.
- Combining the five trophies opens the Silver City.
- Added the Echo of the Veiled Prophet in the Silver City, with 2.5 million life.
- Three lieutenants, healing Hierophants and their Zealots accompany the Veiled Prophet.
- The Veiled Prophet drops Heaven uniques, including Anu&#x27;s Radiance, Inarius&#x27; Wing-Blade and The Wordless.
- The Veiled Prophet also drops the four pieces of Imperius&#x27; set.
- The Wordless has a 4% drop chance from the Veiled Prophet in Hell.
- Cubing The Wordless with runes awakens the amulet in stages.

## The Convergence

- In Harrogath on Hell, cubing the five Echo trophies opens the Black Abyss.
- Added the Convergence encounter, with all five Unborn on one island at once.
- The Unborn are Mephisto, Andariel, Duriel, Diablo and Baal, each with 2.5 million life.
- Lieutenants and guards accompany the Unborn.
- The Unborn drop Convergence uniques, including Worldstone Remnant, Litany of Tathamet and Facet of the Prime Evils.
- Added four growing uniques: The Black Seed, The Faint Mark, The Blood Debt and The Uneasy Dead.
- Five Remnants advance a growing unique to its next stage.
- A Last Breath and five more Remnants advance it again.
- The Black Seed uses Baal&#x27;s Remnants.
- For the other three growing uniques, the Evil whose Remnants you use determines the result.

## The Shade of Diablo

- The Echo of Diablo has a one-in-20 chance to drop the Pentagram of Chaos.
- Tier XV Stone bosses have a one-in-100 chance to drop the Pentagram of Chaos.
- In Harrogath on Hell, cubing a Pentagram of Chaos with three Stones of Jordan opens the Unsealed Sanctuary.
- Added the Shade of Diablo in the Unsealed Sanctuary, with 3 million life, the highest boss life in the game.

## Treasure Vaults and Gilded Fallen

- Hell Stone bosses on tiers IV to XII have a one-in-30 chance to drop the Horadric Vault Sigil.
- Cubing the Horadric Vault Sigil alone opens a sealed Kurast temple.
- The Horadric Vault contains five chests.
- Each chest contains a unique or rare item, two Perfect Gems, a reagent and gold.
- Added the Gilded Wretch to the Horadric Vault, carrying one unique or set item.
- The Gilded Key from Sentinel champions opens the Gilded Vault.
- The Gilded Vault is a sealed cellar with four rooms and 25 Gilded Fallen.
- Each Gilded Fallen in the vault carries gold, gear, runes and a reagent.
- Added lone Gilded Fallen that can appear anywhere in the campaign, on any difficulty.
- Lone Gilded Fallen flee and teleport away if you do not catch them.
- Catching a lone Gilded Fallen awards gold, runes, gems, a reagent and better gear.

## Items and Crafting

### Horadric Reagents

- Added Horadric reagents to monster drops on every difficulty.
- The Quill of Inscription turns a normal item into a magic item.
- The Jawbone of Lies rerolls a magic item.
- The Heart of Ascension turns an item rare.
- The Tail of Chaos rerolls a rare item.
- The Horn of Delving opens every socket an item can hold.
- The Toe of Unmaking strips an item bare.
- The Soul of Legend turns a normal item into a unique item.
- Added three rarer reagents that work on existing items.
- The Eye of Reckoning rerolls the values on a unique or set item, keeping the same item.
- The Claw of Prising removes the runes and gems from a socketed item and returns them to you.
- The Finger of the Council turns a set item into another piece of the same set.

### Sentinel Oils

- Added six oils: Clear, Amber, Crimson, Azure, Black and Golden.
- Cubing an item with a reagent, an oil and a Perfect Gem remakes the item with one guaranteed stat.
- The oil and gem determine the guaranteed stat.
- The reagent determines the item&#x27;s new quality.
- On an amulet, Amber Oil and a Perfect Ruby guarantee +81–100 to Life.
- On an amulet, Azure Oil and a Perfect Diamond guarantee All Resistances +21–30.
- On an amulet, Golden Oil can guarantee +3 to one class&#x27;s skill tree.

### Wax Runes

- Added Xeth, Vot and Myr, three wax runes outside the El-to-Zod chain.
- Xeth grants +5% Faster Cast Rate in a weapon.
- Xeth grants +3% Faster Cast Rate in armor or a shield.
- Vot grants +10% Faster Run/Walk in a weapon.
- Vot grants +5% Faster Run/Walk elsewhere.
- Myr grants +6 Life after each Kill.
- Wax runes drop in the Crypt of the Vigil.
- Wax runes cannot be upgraded in the Cube.
- Added runewords that use wax runes.

### Corruption

- Added Hellforge Embers to Hell champion and unique monster drops, with an approximate one-in-150 drop chance.
- Cubing an Ember with a weapon, armor, ring or amulet of any quality corrupts the item and adds one permanent outcome at once: a new power, or a ruined item.
- Corrupted items cannot use any recipe that changes them.

### Sockets

- Gloves and boots now have a maximum of 2 sockets at item levels 1–40 (previously 0).
- Gloves and boots now have a maximum of 3 sockets at item level 41 and higher (previously 0).
- Belts now have a maximum of 2 sockets at every item level (previously 0).
- Rings now have a maximum of 1 socket at every item level (previously 0).
- These new socket slots accept runewords and gems.
- Warlock grimoires and Necromancer heads now have a maximum of 3 sockets at item level 26 and higher (previously 2).
- The new grimoire and head socket limit accommodates three-rune runewords for those item types.

### Vendors and Gambling

- Gamblers now offer jewels.
- Gamblers now offer all Warlock grimoires.
- Gamblers now offer every dagger type, from the Dagger through its elite forms.
- Akara now sells the Token of Absolution for 100,000 gold.
- Tokens reset skills and stats at any character level.
- The four essences still cube into a Token of Absolution.

### +# to Projectiles

- Added a new item stat, +# to Projectiles.
- One-bolt skills now fan out, like Multiple Shot, with one extra projectile per point: Magic Arrow, Fire Arrow, Cold Arrow, Poison Javelin, Exploding Arrow, Lightning Bolt, Ice Arrow, Plague Javelin, Immolation Arrow, Freezing Arrow, Fire Bolt, Ice Bolt, Ice Blast, Fire Ball, Lightning, Glacial Spike, Frozen Orb, Bone Spear, Molten Boulder, Fire Blast and Miasma Bolt.
- Each extra projectile raises the volley&#x27;s total damage by 35%, shared across the fan.
- Skills that already fire several missiles now fire one more per point, at full damage: Multiple Shot, Teeth, Shock Wave, Charged Strike, Strafe, Lightning Fury, Charged Bolt, Fist of the Heavens, Firestorm, Twister, Echoing Strike, Blade Warp and Miasma Chain.
- Tornado now fires one more tornado per point of +# to Projectiles, and its total damage rises 35% per extra tornado, shared across them.
- Each affected skill&#x27;s description shows how many projectiles it fires.
- With no +# to Projectiles, every skill fires exactly what it fired before.

## Uniques, Sets and Runewords

- Added 417 uniques, from Normal through the deepest endgame: 291 unique items, 81 unique charms and 45 unique jewels.
- Added 21 sets, including two-piece levelling sets.
- Added 129 runewords.
- Unique and set items can now drop more than once in the same game.
- Every new unique has a line of lore.
- The Echoes, Stone bosses, Sentinels, Seal ubers, Unborn and Veiled Prophet each have their own uniques.
- Added eleven Leaves, all unique small charms.
- Cubing a Leaf with two other unique small charms binds it into a Journal, a stronger charm.
- Herbs of the Great Oak grant their effects while carried, like charms.
- Bishibosh, Rakanishu and Bartuc the Bloody each have a one-in-25 chance to drop their own unique alongside their usual loot.
- Eighteen named monsters, including Radament, the Summoner and the Council, can drop their own unique jewels in Nightmare and Hell.
- The Countess&#x27;s unique jewel drops only in Hell.
- Uber Mephisto, Diablo and Baal now drop loot in Uber Tristram.
- Each of the three Uber Tristram bosses can drop his own amulet.
- Lilith can now drop Lilith&#x27;s Gaze alongside her usual Pandemonium loot.
- Uber Izual can now drop Izual&#x27;s Betrayal alongside his usual Pandemonium loot.
- Added Hail (Tal Ort Sol, helms, level 27): +1–2 to Projectiles, +10% Faster Cast Rate, +10% Increased Attack Speed.
- Added Deluge (Lem Ist Gul, helms and gloves, level 53): +1 to All Skills, +3–5 to Projectiles, All Resistances +15.

## Player Balance

- Running now drains stamina at 60% of the Diablo II rate for every class (previously 100%).
- A full stamina bar now supports about two-thirds more running time (previously the Diablo II duration).
- Every skill now accepts up to 25 hard points (previously 20).
