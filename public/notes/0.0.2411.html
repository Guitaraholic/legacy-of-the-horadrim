0.0.2411 · More ways to find gear

20 October 2026 · Retrospective highlights

More endgame equipment joins ordinary drops, alongside new Stone map layouts.

0.0.2411 · Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.2300.

New

Twenty-two endgame equipment variants can now drop from level-eligible monsters as well as their named bosses.

Their existing boss sources remain available.

The five Canticle variants and forty-six set pieces can also appear in ordinary level-eligible drops.

Current reference page: Mercenaries →

Black Canyon Mines gains a long mining cut, while Wortham Bluffs and the Canyons of Dahlgur receive new layouts.

Current reference page: Stones →

The Fractured Halls gains two cracked Worldstone Keep layouts and Ithmar the Waykeeper as its end boss.
