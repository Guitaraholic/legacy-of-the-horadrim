# 0.0.3983 — Burrows of Pain rebuilt

Burrows of Pain 4 to 7 are rebuilt as green-lit spider caves whose passages line up, and the wiki&#x27;s skill pages show what +# to Projectiles does.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3976.

## Other fixes

- Burrows of Pain Levels 4 to 7 are rebuilt from cave passages that always line up, so there are no more invisible walls or tunnels that end in rock. They keep the Spider Cavern&#x27;s feel: green light, spiders, cocoon chests and egg sacs, webs and webbed bodies on the floor. The stairs have moved, and both can always be reached from where you arrive.

- The wiki&#x27;s skill and Class balance pages now show which skills fan out or fire more with +# to Projectiles.
