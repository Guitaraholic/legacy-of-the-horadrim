# 0.0.3022 — Oil crafting

Sentinel oils now craft: a reagent, an oil, a Perfect Gem and an item give a crafted item with a guaranteed stat.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.3012.

## New

- Cube a reagent, a Sentinel oil, a Perfect Gem and an item to craft a new item with a guaranteed stat. The reagent sets the result&#x27;s quality, the oil sets its role and the gem picks the stat.

## Other fixes

- Each oil&#x27;s tooltip now names its role and says to cube it with a reagent, a Perfect Gem and an item.
