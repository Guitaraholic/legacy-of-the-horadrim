# 0.3.1 — Jewel gambling, Talic, a tidier loot filter and new quivers

A small update to 0.3.0: Jewels can be gambled again, Talic spins again, the loot filter gives Legacy of the Horadrim items their own tabs, and the four quiver families get their own icons.

Public · Compared with 0.3.0.

## Contents

- [Items and Crafting](#items-and-crafting)
- [User Interface](#user-interface)
- [Public Fixes](#public-fixes)
- [Bug Fixes](#bug-fixes)

## Items and Crafting

### Quivers

- Hexed, Flight, Anointed and Graven Arrows and Bolts now have their own inventory icons instead of a tinted vanilla quiver.

## User Interface

- The loot filter&#x27;s Legacy of the Horadrim Uber Materials tab is now five tabs: Stone Seals, Descent Sigils, The Last Vigil, The Convergence and Heaven&#x27;s Ascent.
- Added Stone Fragments and Crafting Oils loot filter tabs. The five Stone Fragments move there from Horadric Currency.
- The Sealed Breaths tab is now Remnants / Sealed Breaths. The five Remnants move there from the Essence / Token of Absolution tab.
- The Horadric Vault Sigil, the Gilded Key and Gutted Scrap now sit in the Keys tab.
- The Pentagram of Chaos now sits in the Uber Materials tab with the other Uber keys and organs.
- Binding Blades now have their own entry in the Swords tab, so they can be shown or hidden.
- The Terrorize Tokens and Essence / Token of Absolution tabs now use three columns.

## Public Fixes

- Fixed a bug where buying a Jewel from a gambler froze the game on &quot;Waiting for confirmation of transaction&quot;. [Public issue #17](https://github.com/Guitaraholic/legacy-of-the-horadrim/issues/17)

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed a bug where Talic, one of the Ancients on Arreat Summit, froze in place when he used Whirlwind. He spins and fights again, with his usual damage.
- Fixed a bug where the loot filter&#x27;s Runebands tab listed none of the sixteen Runebands.
