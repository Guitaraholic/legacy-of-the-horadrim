# 0.0.4068 — Corruption in one step

The Hellforge Ember now corrupts an item and shows its result at once, Ruined items carry penalties instead of being re-rolled, and Descent stairs sit far apart.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.4050.

## Other fixes

- Corrupting an item with the Hellforge Ember now adds its result in the same transmute. There&#x27;s no second transmute to reveal it, and cubing a corrupted item alone does nothing.

- On every Descent floor the stairs up and down now sit far apart, so each floor is crossed rather than skipped. Cinders of Hatred Levels 1 to 3 are smaller, at 20 rooms each.

- The busiest Descent floors carry fewer decorative objects in HD, to stay under the load the Stone maps were proven at.

## Balance

- A Ruined corruption now keeps the item&#x27;s stats and adds penalties for its slot, instead of re-rolling the item into a rare.

### Corruption: the Ruined outcome

| Stat | Condition | Before | After | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ruined armour | 10% of corruptions | Re-rolled into a rare | Keeps its stats, -20% defence, -10% all resistances | The item survives with a penalty |
| Ruined weapon | 10% of corruptions | Re-rolled into a rare | Keeps its stats, -20% damage, -10% all resistances | The item survives with a penalty |
| Ruined ring or amulet | 10% of corruptions | Re-rolled into a rare | Keeps its stats, -32 life, -15% all resistances | The item survives with a penalty |

**Why:** A Ruined item now loses what its slot could have gained, rather than being replaced.
