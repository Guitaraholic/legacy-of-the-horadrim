0.0.2460 · At the pinnacle

20 October 2026 · Retrospective highlights

The endgame branches into pinnacle encounters, with named rewards and more reasons to return.

0.0.2460 · Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.1448.

New

Pinnacle encounters give the endgame its highest targets.

The Convergence brings the five Unborn together in the Black Abyss. The Echoes of the Descent supply the trophies for its portal.

Current reference page: Convergence →

Seals open named encounters across the endgame.

Face the Avatar of Azmodan in the Court of Sin, seek the true Avatar of Belial in the Hall of Lies, or challenge Vardek and Sevra together in the Ossuary of the Bonebound. Bartuc and Zoltun Kulle have their own sealed arenas. Their five trophies lead toward the Echo of the Veiled Prophet.

Current reference page: Sigils →

The Last Breaths and Canticles give named fights their own rewards.

Earlier private builds already contained the five act-boss Last Breath charms and five Canticle variants.

Andariel’s Last Breath is one of the five Last Breath charms.
