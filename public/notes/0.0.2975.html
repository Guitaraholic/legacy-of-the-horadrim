0.0.2975 · Crypt crash fix and map repairs

22 October 2026 · Retrospective highlights

The Crypt of the Vigil crash is fixed, and five maps get repairs: Moonwood, Bastion Walls, the Menagerie of Fahir, the Horadric Vault and the Canyons of Dahlgur.

0.0.2975 · Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.2962.

Other fixesBalance

Other fixes

The Crypt of the Vigil no longer crashes after the second Sentinel shade.

In the Moonwood, the river now runs off the map's upper-right edge, and the false wall on the minimap is gone.

On the Bastion Walls, the lower-right edge drops away into fog, and the middle hut area is snow with a frozen pool.

In the Menagerie of Fahir, a stack of crates now covers the old trapdoor hole.

The Horadric Vault no longer has blood on its floors and walls.

In the Canyons of Dahlgur, the seams beside the mesas are filled in.

Balance

Vigil and Sentinel bosses' Holy Fire

The Vigil and Sentinel bosses now use the monster versions of Holy Freeze and Holy Fire, which fixes the crash. Holy Fire hits a little softer.
