# 0.0.3012 — Runecraft specials

Three new special currencies join the Runecraft tab around the cube, the reagent column becomes demon body parts with an upgrade ladder, and the Canyons of Dahlgur ground is smoothed.

Pre-release (private alpha) · Compared with 0.0.2994.

## New

- Three special currencies sit around the Horadric Cube. The Claw of Prising frees every gem, rune and jewel from a socketed item and destroys the item. The Eye of Reckoning rerolls a unique or set item&#x27;s stats. The Finger of the Council turns a set item into a different piece of the same set.

- The Runecraft reagents are now demon body parts. Three of one upgrade to one of the next, and three specials make one random special.

## Other fixes

- The open sand in the Canyons of Dahlgur is one even surface, and you can walk up to the feet of the rocks.
