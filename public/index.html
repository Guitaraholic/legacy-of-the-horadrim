What Legacy of the Horadrim adds

The Descent

50 floors in five bands, each ending in an Echo; the deepest three Echoes outmatch the Ubers.

Pinnacle fights

14 echoes and 77 new bosses, among them the Echoes of Lazarus, Red Vex, Black Jade and Tal Rasha, each with drops of its own.

Sanctuary Stones

Cube a stone with a gem for an outdoor Hell map: fifteen tiers, 45 places, each with its own curse.

New gear

510 new uniques, 32 new sets and 191 new runewords, with Leaves that bind into Journals.

Class rebalance

All eight classes retuned, skills to 25 hard points, and every changed number shown.

Skill effects

10 new item modifiers that reshape skills: Companion Vitality, Faster Pulse Rate, Repeated Attacks, and more.
